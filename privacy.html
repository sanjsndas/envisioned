    Last updated: April 15, 2025

    At Envisioned Living Spaces, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple and transparent.

    1. Information we collect

    We collect information to provide a better reading experience and to communicate with you effectively. The types of information we collect fall into three categories:

    Information you give us voluntarily

    When you subscribe to our newsletter, submit a contact form, or pitch a story, you may provide us with:

    • Personal identifiers: your name, email address, and (optionally) your phone number.
    • Professional details: your company, role, or portfolio links when submitting a pitch or partnership inquiry.
    • Communication content: the message, feedback, or files you send us.

    Information collected automatically

    Like most websites, we use analytics tools to understand how readers find and use our content. This may include:

    • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, links clicked, and referring URLs.
    • Device & browser data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and screen size.
    • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (typically at city or region level).

    Information from third parties

    If you interact with our content on social media or through embedded services (e.g., YouTube, Instagram), those platforms may share limited data with us, subject to their own privacy policies.

    2. How we use your information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To respond to inquiries: answering your emails, pitches, and feedback.
    • To send newsletters: delivering the weekly digest you opted into. You can unsubscribe at any time.
    • To improve our site: analyzing which articles resonate and optimizing layout and navigation.
    • To protect our rights: detecting and preventing fraud, spam, or abuse.
    • To comply with the law: meeting legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

    We never sell your personal data. We do not rent, trade, or sell your email address or any other personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

    3. Cookies & tracking

    Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember your preferences and to measure site performance. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

    You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the site, but the core content remains accessible.

    4. Third-party services

    We work with a small number of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf. These include:

    Provider Purpose Data shared
    Google Analytics Website traffic analysis IP address, usage data (anonymized)
    Mailchimp Newsletter delivery Name, email address, engagement data
    Cloudflare Security & performance IP address, request metadata
    Unsplash Embedded imagery None (images served via CDN)

    Each provider is contractually bound to protect your data and may only use it for the specific purpose we've agreed upon. We encourage you to review their privacy policies for more information.

    5. Data security

    We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

    • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
    • Regular security audits and software updates.
    • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

    However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    6. Your rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Deletion: request that we delete your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
    • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
    • Objection: object to certain processing activities, such as direct marketing.
    • Withdraw consent: unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time via the link in every email.

    To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@envisionedliving.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

    7. Children's privacy

    Our content is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

    8. Changes to this policy

    We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    If we make material changes, we will notify newsletter subscribers via email and/or place a prominent notice on our site.

    9. Contact us

    If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

    Email: privacy@envisionedliving.com

    Mail: Envisioned Living Spaces, Attn: Privacy Officer, 1201 Design District, Suite 4B, Portland, OR 97209

    Response time: Within 30 days for formal requests.
