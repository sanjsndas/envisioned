    Your rights, our responsibilities, and how we handle your data — in plain language.

    Last updated: March 2025

    Welcome to Envisioned Living Spaces. This page explains the terms under which we provide our website and content, and how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. We've tried to keep this clear and readable — no unnecessary legalese.

    In short: We respect your privacy. We collect only what we need to run the site and improve it. We never sell your data. You can opt out of our newsletter at any time. By using this site, you agree to the terms below.

    1. Who We Are

    Envisioned Living Spaces ("we", "us", "our") is an independent editorial journal covering interior design, architecture, materials, and modern living. Our website is www.envisionedlivingspaces.com.

    If you have any questions about these terms or your data, you can reach us at hello@envisionedliving.com.

    2. Acceptance of Terms

    By accessing or using our website, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Privacy. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please do not use the site.

    We may update these terms from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

    3. Information We Collect

    3.1 Information you provide voluntarily

    We collect personal information only when you choose to give it to us. This includes:

    • Newsletter subscription: Your email address when you sign up for our weekly digest.
    • Contact forms: Your name, email address, and the content of your message when you contact us.
    • Comments or contributions: Any information you submit if you write for us or engage with our editorial team.

    3.2 Information collected automatically

    Like most websites, we collect certain technical information automatically when you visit. This may include:

    • Your IP address (anonymized where possible).
    • Browser type and version.
    • Pages you visit and time spent on them.
    • Referring website or source.
    • Device type and operating system.

    This data is aggregated and used to understand how our site is used, so we can improve it. It is not used to identify you personally.

    4. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To send you our newsletter (if you have subscribed).
    • To respond to your enquiries and messages.
    • To analyze site traffic and improve our content and user experience.
    • To comply with legal obligations, if required.

    We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

    5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

    • Consent: When you subscribe to our newsletter or voluntarily submit information through a form.
    • Legitimate interests: To analyze site usage and improve our services, provided this does not override your rights.
    • Legal obligation: Where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

    6. Cookies and Tracking

    We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

    You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site.

    7. Sharing Your Information

    We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

    • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services (such as email newsletter platforms and analytics providers) that process data on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.
    • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process, we may disclose your information to the relevant authorities.
    • Business transfers: In the unlikely event that we merge with or are acquired by another organization, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

    8. Data Retention

    We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in these terms:

    • Newsletter subscriptions: We keep your email address until you unsubscribe, after which we may retain a suppression record to ensure we do not contact you again.
    • Contact enquiries: We retain correspondence for up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for legal or operational reasons.
    • Analytics data: Aggregated analytics data is retained for up to 26 months.

    9. Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Right to access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Right to erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
    • Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
    • Right to data portability: You can request that we transfer your data to another organization or to you.
    • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
    • Right to withdraw consent: If we process your data based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@envisionedliving.com. We will respond within 30 days.

    If you are in the EEA and believe we have not handled your data in accordance with the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    10. Data Security

    We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to data, and regular reviews of our security practices.

    However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    11. International Data Transfers

    Our website is hosted on servers that may be located outside your country of residence. If you are in the EEA, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries that have not been deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection. In such cases, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

    12. Children's Privacy

    Our website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.
