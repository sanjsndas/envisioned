    Our Story

    Envisioned Living Spaces began in 2019 as a small newsletter shared among a handful of architects, designers, and curious homeowners. What united them was a shared frustration: too much design writing felt either shallow or needlessly academic. There was room for something in between—rigorous, readable, and genuinely useful.

    Today, we publish long-form articles, case studies, and practical guides for people who care about how spaces are made. Our readers include design professionals, students, and homeowners who want to understand the "why" behind the "what."

    “Good design writing should leave you seeing your own home differently—not just wishing you had someone else's.”

    What We Cover

    Our editorial focus spans the full spectrum of residential design:

    • Interior Design — color, texture, furniture, and the sensory experience of rooms.
    • Architecture — residential building, renovations, and the relationship between structure and site.
    • Materials — from lime plaster to reclaimed timber, and how material choices shape atmosphere.
    • Lighting — layered lighting strategies, daylighting, and the psychology of light.
    • Space Planning — circulation, proportion, and the quiet logic of well-planned rooms.
    • Modern Homes — case studies of sustainable, passive, and thoughtfully designed houses.

    Our Approach

    We believe that design writing should be accessible without being simplistic. Every article is grounded in research—interviews with architects, visits to completed projects, and a close reading of the materials and methods involved. We avoid trend-chasing for its own sake, and we never publish sponsored content disguised as editorial.

    We are particularly interested in the quiet, enduring principles of good design: proportion, light, balance, and material honesty. These ideas outlast any trend, and they are central to what we write.

    A passive house project featured in our case study series.

    Editorial Independence

    Envisioned Living Spaces is independently owned and operated. We do not accept payment for coverage, and our editorial decisions are made solely by our editors. When we recommend a product or material, it is because we believe it is genuinely worth considering—not because of any commercial relationship.

    Our small team is based across Europe and North America, which gives us a broad perspective on regional design traditions and emerging practices.

    What Guides Us

    Clarity

    We write for people, not for search engines. Every article aims to be clear, useful, and a pleasure to read.

    Independence

    No sponsored content, no pay-for-play. Our recommendations are based on research and experience alone.

    Sustainability

    We pay close attention to material sourcing, energy performance, and the long-term impact of design decisions.

    Craft

    We admire good detailing—the junction of materials, the quality of light, the care taken in construction.

    Work With Us

    We are always interested in hearing from architects, designers, and makers with projects worth sharing. If you have a completed home, a material innovation, or a perspective that would enrich our pages, please get in touch.

    For advertising and partnership enquiries, please contact us separately—we keep editorial and commercial conversations entirely distinct.
