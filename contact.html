    Frequently Asked Questions

    How quickly do you respond to messages?
    We aim to reply to every message within two business days. For time-sensitive press inquiries, please mark your subject line “URGENT” and we’ll prioritize it.
    Can I submit a guest article or pitch?
    Absolutely. We welcome pitches from architects, designers, and writers. Please send a short summary (150–200 words) along with your credentials and a link to previous work. Use the subject “Pitch: [topic]”.
    Do you accept advertising or sponsored content?
    We work with a limited number of partners whose values align with our editorial mission. All sponsored content is clearly labeled. Reach out to partners@envisionedliving.com for our media kit.
    I’m a homeowner — can you feature my project?
    We love featuring thoughtful residential projects. Send a few high-resolution photos, a brief description, and the architect or designer’s name. We’ll follow up if it’s a good fit.
    How do I report an error or correction?
    Accuracy matters. Please email corrections@envisionedliving.com with the article title, URL, and the specific correction. We’ll review and update promptly.
    Can I republish your content?
    You may quote up to two paragraphs with a link back to the original article. For full syndication or translation requests, please contact us directly.
